Artwork specification

FormatVector preferred - SVG, PDF or EPS with outlined type. Otherwise PNG at 300dpi at final size, with transparency.
ColourCMYK-safe. Anything outside CMYK gamut will be matched as closely as the printer allows; neon and metallics will not reproduce.
BleedAdd 5mm bleed on every edge. Keep type and logos 8mm inside the trim.
ContrastThe car is silver. Pure white artwork disappears; give white marks a keyline or a background.
File namesZONEID-brand.svg, e.g. H5-acme.svg, so nothing gets applied to the wrong panel.
DeadlineFiles are due 48 hours after the auction closes. Miss it and the zone is re-offered to the next bidder.

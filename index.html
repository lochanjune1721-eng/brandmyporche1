FAQ

Is this real?+

Yes. Real car, real zones, real bids. The only absurd part is the premise that a 911 is ad inventory — everything downstream of that is straightforward.

Why are the XS zones so cheap?+

Because a board whose floor is $800 sits empty on day one, and an empty board never gets anyone to bid $6,000. The six XS zones are $250, they live in the contingency row across the nose, and they go to running costs — fuel, wrap, wash — not the car fund. Everything else buys the car. That is why they sit outside the $135,000.

How does payment work?+

You do not pay to bid. When the auction closes, the top bid on each zone wins and I send a payment link. Money moves after winning, never before.

What if someone outbids me?+

You get an email with a one-click link back. The minimum raise is $50 over the standing bid.

What artwork do you need?+

Vector (SVG, PDF, EPS) or a 300dpi PNG with transparency, CMYK-safe. Every zone's exact size in centimetres is in the media kit. Files are due 48 hours after the close.

Can any brand join?+

Almost. No gambling, no crypto pump-and-dumps, no adult content, nothing I would be embarrassed to explain to a Porsche technician or to my mother. I keep final say and will refund rather than argue.

Why 88 zones?+

Because that is how many fit on the bodywork at these six sizes without crossing a shut line, a door handle, a wheel arch, a light or the number plate. The count came out of the car, not the spreadsheet — and then the prices were set so the total landed exactly on the invoice.

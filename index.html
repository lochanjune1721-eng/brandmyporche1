FAQ

Is this real?+

Yes. Real car, real zones, real bids. The only absurd part is the premise that a 911 is ad inventory — everything downstream of that is straightforward.

How does payment work?+

Give me your website and your icon, and pay through PayPal. That is the whole form — your name and email come from PayPal, so you never type them. The price is set on my server rather than in your browser, so what you see is what you are charged.

What if two of us want the same zone?+

First to pay gets it. While you are at the PayPal checkout that zone is reserved and nobody else can start buying it; if you back out, or take too long, it goes straight back on the board. Nothing is ever sold twice.

Can I get a refund?+

Until the wrap goes on, yes — email me and I will refund it, and the zone returns to the board automatically. After it has been printed and applied, no, because it has been.

What artwork do you need?+

Vector (SVG, PDF, EPS) or a 300dpi PNG with transparency, CMYK-safe. Every zone's exact size in centimetres is in the media kit. Files are due 48 hours after the close.

Can any brand join?+

Almost. No gambling, no crypto pump-and-dumps, no adult content, nothing I would be embarrassed to explain to a Porsche technician or to my mother. I keep final say, and if I say no you are refunded in full, same day.

Why 82 zones?+

Because that is how many fit on the bodywork at these five sizes without crossing a shut line, a door handle, a wheel arch, a light or the number plate, and without any of them being too small to be worth selling. The count came out of the car, not the spreadsheet — and then the prices were set so the total landed exactly on the invoice.

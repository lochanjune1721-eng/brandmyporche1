FAQ

Is this real? +

Yes. The car is real, the zones are real, the bids are real. The only fictional thing is the idea that a Porsche isn't ad inventory.

What do I actually get? +

Your logo on the panel for 14 days, in every photo and video, plus a listed spot with a backlink here. No guaranteed impressions. Guaranteed it goes everywhere the car goes.

How does payment work? +

You don't pay to bid. When the auction closes, top bid per zone wins. I send a Stripe link. Payment happens after winning, never before.

What if someone outbids me? +

You get an email with a one-click link back to outbid. Minimum increment is $50 over the current top.

Can any brand join? +

Almost. No gambling, no crypto pump-and-dumps, no adult content, nothing I'd be embarrassed to explain to a Porsche tech or my mother. I keep final say.

Why are XS spots so cheap? +

XS spots are $250 and sit outside the $135,000 maths — they go to the running costs. Everything else buys the car. We added them because a board whose floor is $800 stays empty on day one; cheap spots are what make the page look alive, and an alive page is what makes someone bid $6,000.

Why are you doing this? +

Because the reference — brandmymac.com — sold out 10 MacBook spots at 109% of goal with ~17k visitors by treating an absurd premise with total seriousness. This is the same mechanic, on a surface you can spin.
